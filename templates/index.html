High Risk — Immediate Action Required

This patient's clinical parameters indicate elevated cardiovascular risk. Please take the following steps:

See a Cardiologist
Schedule an urgent appointment with a heart specialist for further diagnostic testing and evaluation immediately.
Follow Medical Advice
Take all prescribed medications as directed. Never stop any medication without first consulting your doctor.
Stop Smoking
Stop smoking immediately and reduce alcohol consumption. These significantly worsen cardiovascular risk.
Diet Modification
Reduce salt, saturated fats, and processed foods. Consult a dietitian for a personalised heart-healthy meal plan.
Monitor Daily
Monitor your blood pressure and heart rate every day. Keep a record to share with your doctor at each visit.
Light Activity Only
Avoid strenuous exercise until cleared by a doctor. Light walking under medical supervision may be permitted.

Low Risk — Keep Up The Great Work!

No significant indicators of heart disease detected. Here is how to maintain your excellent heart health:

Heart-Healthy Foods
Eat fruits, vegetables, whole grains, fish (salmon, mackerel), nuts, olive oil, oats, berries, and avocado daily.
Stay Active
Aim for at least 30 minutes of moderate exercise (walking, cycling, swimming) at least 5 days per week.
Stay Hydrated
Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily. Proper hydration supports healthy blood circulation and heart function.
Quality Sleep
Aim for 7 to 9 hours of quality sleep per night. Poor sleep significantly increases cardiovascular risk over time.
Manage Stress
Practice relaxation techniques such as meditation, deep breathing, or yoga to keep chronic stress levels low.
🩺
Regular Checkups
Visit your doctor at least once a year for routine heart health screenings — even when you feel perfectly healthy.
Avoid Smoking
If you smoke, quit immediately. Smoking significantly increases cardiovascular risk even in currently low-risk patients.
Daily Superfoods
Garlic, dark chocolate (70%+), green tea, leafy greens (spinach, kale), and legumes all actively support heart health.
